XPND / The platform

XPND is the introduction layer for industrial sourcing

It brings the requirement, the capability and the decision into one place, so an organisation can reach a company it has no record of, judge whether it can be trusted, and defend the choice afterwards.

Your sourcing system ranks the suppliers you have

The constraint

A company that solved your problem in another sector holds no record with you, so the search cannot return it. And a supplier with no history is hard to defend on paper, so the work goes back to the one you already use.

How an introduction is made

Five steps
  1. 01 Posted

    Say what you need, without your name on it

    The post carries the problem. It does not carry you. ConvergX alone holds the name behind it.

  2. 02 Answered

    Three ways for the room to answer

    Participate, Feedback or Partnership. A member answers the requirement without being told whose it is.

  3. 03 Read

    The two sides never meet each other

    The side asking and the side answering do not see one another. A person at ConvergX is the only thing that joins them.

  4. 04 Decided

    The review ends in a decision

    ConvergX asks the questions rather than checking a record. What comes out is a call: introduced, or not taken forward.

  5. 05 Told

    Know when your post moves

    The alert carries no content of its own, so nothing about a requirement leaves the platform.

No open registration and no account to create. Access is decided one request at a time.

Two ways in

One door for a requirement your own supply base cannot fill. One for a capability already in service.
