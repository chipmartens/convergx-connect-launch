Why a sourcing search returns the suppliers you have

The constraint

Every sourcing system an organisation owns ranks companies that already hold a record with it. A company that has solved the hard version of your requirement somewhere else, in a sector you do not buy from, is not in that ranking, because it has never held a contract with you. The search is doing what it was built to do, and it cannot return that company.

The second constraint sits after the search. When a capable company is found and has no history with the organisation, selecting it is difficult to set down on paper and defend, so the work returns to the supplier who was always going to receive it. Both constraints are structural, and neither is solved by searching harder.

What ConvergX does with a requirement

The method

ConvergX begins at the requirement rather than the supplier list. An organisation describes what it needs solved and the industries it already sources from. A company describes a capability it has in service today and where that capability is running. A person at ConvergX reads both, decides which belongs in front of which, and puts the organisation's name on the introduction.

The requirement side

You describe what you need solved. You do not have to say it in public, and you do not have to name yourself to the room while you are asking.

The capability side

You describe what you have built and where it is already in service. ConvergX reads for capability running today rather than for the sector it was built for.

There is no open registration on this side and no account to create. Access is decided one request at a time, and ConvergX puts its name on the introduction it makes.

What the platform holds

In the app
The Opportunity Board

A requirement, posted without a name on it

A post carries the problem, the project or the need, and not the organisation behind it. Other members answer with Participate, Feedback or Partnership. ConvergX alone holds the name behind a post, and decides which answers reach it.

Alerts

Told when a post moves

Interest in something you posted, or a follow-up from ConvergX, arrives as an alert. The alert carries no content of its own, so nothing about a requirement travels outside the platform.

  • Who is in it Members admitted one request at a time, across the industries ConvergX convenes. There is no public directory and no member list to browse.
  • On a phone The platform reaches members by invitation after access is granted. Signing in afterwards is a link sent to your email, so there is no password to carry.

Two ways in

An organisation with a requirement its own supply base cannot fill comes in through one door. A company with a capability already in service comes in through the other. Both are read by a person, and both are answered.
