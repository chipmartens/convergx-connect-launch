XPND / The platform

XPND is the introduction layer for industrial sourcing

It brings the requirement, the capability and the decision into one place, so an organisation can reach a company it has no record of, judge whether it can be trusted, and defend the choice afterwards.

Request access How it works

Your sourcing system ranks the suppliers you have

The constraint

A company that solved your problem in another sector holds no record with you, so the search cannot return it. And a supplier with no history is hard to defend on paper, so the work goes back to the one you already use.

How an introduction is made

Three steps
  1. 01 Posted

    A requirement, with no name on it

    You say what you need solved. Not in public, and not under your own name. ConvergX alone holds the name behind a post.

  2. 02 Read

    One person reads both sides

    An organisation says what it needs. A company says what it has running today. A person at ConvergX reads both and decides which belongs in front of which.

  3. 03 Decided

    A person decides, and says so

    ConvergX asks the questions and makes the call. There is no score and no ranking. The introduction it makes leaves a record you can defend the decision with.

No open registration and no account to create. Access is decided one request at a time.
