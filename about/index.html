Who we are

The organisation

ConvergX began with a simple observation from our Founder, Dame Kimberley Van Vliet: organizations across different industries were often facing similar challenges, yet solving them in isolation, duplicating effort, increasing costs, and missing opportunities to learn from one another.

The challenge was rarely a lack of solutions. More often, it was a lack of connection. Organizations struggled to identify relevant expertise outside their own sectors, understand how solutions could be adapted to new environments, and navigate the path from capability to implementation.

What began as a curated cross-sector forum for senior decision-makers focused on real-world operational challenges has evolved into a global platform for collaboration, commercialization, and problem-solving. Today, ConvergX delivers an international Xchange, regional activations, and ConvergX Xpand, our consulting practice supporting clients across defence, aerospace, energy, mining, manufacturing, agriculture, aviation, and emerging technologies.

At our core, we help organizations discover proven solutions, build strategic partnerships, and accelerate action on the challenges that matter most.

Dame Kimberley Van Vliet, Founder of ConvergX

Kimberley Van Vliet

Founder & CEO
ConvergX

Dame Kimberley Van Vliet is a leading voice in Canada's innovation and defence ecosystem, known for bridging commercial and military sectors across aerospace, defence, security, energy, mining, agriculture, construction, and advanced technologies.

Over 16 years ago, she founded WāVv, a group of companies accelerating cross-industry innovation worldwide. She's also the creator of ConvergX Xpand, a global consulting practice, and ConvergX Global Congress, the world's first Congress to unite decision-makers across industries to solve problems they didn't know they had, with solutions from sectors they never expected could help.

Kimberley serves as Deputy Chair for NIAG's Community of Interest with Allied Command Transformation (ACT), focusing on Innovation, C4ISR, and Logistics & Sustainment, and works closely with NATO divisions including NCIA, NSPA, STO, and DIANA, regularly engaging with NATO HQ, NORAD, and USNORTHCOM.

Recognized among Canada's Top 20 Women in Defence and a 2022 Governor General's Canadian Leadership Conference delegate, Kimberley brings a strategic, cross-industry perspective to Canada's defence and security priorities globally.

Outside of work, she's a proud Mum, a licensed private pilot, and a certified Divemaster.

Chris Van Vliet

SME & Project lead
ConvergX Global Solutions Foundation

A Subject Matter Expert and Project lead at ConvergX Global Solutions Foundation, Chris was born and raised on a family farm in Edmonton, Alberta. He graduated with Honors from Brentwood College School and the University of Alberta before serving nearly 25 years in the Royal Canadian Air Force, followed by a second career as a pilot at WestJet Airlines.

His RCAF career took him from Alaska to Texas and from the Comox Valley to Turkey, flying aircraft from helicopters to jets; he was a member of the Snowbirds, Canada's Military Air Demonstration Team, later serving as Canada's Senior National Representative to the Euro-NATO Joint Jet Pilot Training Program at Sheppard AFB, Texas, as an advanced jet training instructor and instructor trainer, with roles including Snowbird Squadron Operations Officer and Commander of the RCAF Flight Instructor School.

He speaks Spanish and French in addition to English, and for the past 17 years has advised, and now consults for ConvergX Global Solutions Foundation in Defense and Aerospace. Chris has been married for over 40 years, and they have two adult children.
