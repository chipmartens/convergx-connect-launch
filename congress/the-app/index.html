Congress app / Sep 22 to 24

Your agenda lives in the app.

Sessions, the people in the room and the meetings ConvergX brokers sit alongside it, and each one updates as the programme changes.

How to get in

ConvergX sends confirmed delegates an invitation link before the doors open. Open it on the phone you will carry in Calgary.

Where the app stops

Push and offline
  • Push notifications They reach Android phones, and iPhones receive none. On an iPhone, opening the app shows what has changed. The agenda and the board are identical on both.
  • Offline The agenda you already loaded still opens. Nothing new arrives until the network returns.
