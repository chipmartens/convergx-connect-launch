01
At the Congress

Three views, and the app decides which one matters

Every delegate at the Congress has the same three. The programme they are following, the Board where the room posts what it needs, and the alerts that say when either has changed.

The programme, as it stands today

Agenda

Every session, speaker, track and room for the three days, with a My agenda view of the sessions you have chosen. When the organisers move a session, the agenda updates itself, and the meetings ConvergX arranges for you sit alongside it.

Post what you need without saying who you are

Opportunity Board

Posts on the Board are anonymous to other delegates, and ConvergX alone holds the name behind one. Another delegate can answer with Participate, Feedback or Partnership, and ConvergX brokers the introduction from there.

Told when something changes

Alerts

A rescheduled session, a cancelled one, interest in your post, or a follow-up from an organiser arrives as an alert. The alert itself carries no content, so nothing about your posts leaves the app.

How you get in

Three steps
  1. Step 01

    Register for the Congress

    Registration is what puts you on the invitation list.
  2. Step 02

    Watch for the invitation

    ConvergX emails confirmed delegates an invitation link before Sep 22, at the address used to register. Open it on the phone you will carry in Calgary.
  3. Step 03

    Set up your account once

    After that, signing in is a link sent to your email. There is no password to remember on a conference network.

Where the app stops

Push and offline
  • Push notifications They reach Android phones today. On an iPhone, opening the app shows what has changed, and the agenda and the Board are identical on both.
  • Offline The agenda you already loaded still opens. Nothing new arrives until the network returns.
02
The rest of the year

One door for a requirement, one for a capability

The Congress runs for three days. The work it starts runs the rest of the year, on the same platform, and it comes in through two doors. An organisation with a requirement its own supply base cannot fill uses one. A company with a capability already in service uses the other.

The requirement side

You describe what you need solved and the industries you already source from. You do not have to say it in public, and you do not have to name yourself to the room.

The capability side

You describe what you have built and where it is already running. ConvergX is reading for capability in service today rather than for the sector it was built for.

A person at ConvergX reads each one and decides. There is no open registration on this side and no account to create: access is decided one request at a time, and ConvergX puts its name on the introduction.
